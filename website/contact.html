Before you ask

For express requests we target collection within 90 minutes inside the Randstad and the same day across the Benelux and Ruhr. Standard road freight is normally collected within 24 hours of a confirmed booking, depending on the lane and the vehicle class.

Yes. We are AEO authorised and run an in-house brokerage desk for T1, T2, EX and IM declarations. We will also prepare the CMR, packing lists and certificates of origin where the lane requires them, so nothing waits at the border for a missing document.

We operate reefer and dual-compartment trailers holding 2–8 °C or −20 °C, with continuous temperature logging and a certificate issued per consignment. Pharma lanes follow our GDP procedures; food lanes follow HACCP hygiene protocols.

Standard CMR liability applies to every international consignment. Additional all-risk cover can be arranged per shipment or as an annual policy — tell us the cargo value in your enquiry and we will include the premium in the quote.
