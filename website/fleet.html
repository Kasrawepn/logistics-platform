Maintenance & Safety

A workshop, not a repair account

Two in-house workshops and a network of contracted partners keep our fleet inside PMI on schedule. Vehicles are inspected against a checklist before every departure, not once a year.

  • 92% serviced in-house, with safety-critical work always under our own supervision.
  • Driver defect reporting by app, each entry closed out with a named engineer.
  • Security screening and continuous CPC training for every driver we put on the road.
Sustainability

Fewer litres for the same delivery

We do not publish pledges we cannot evidence. What we can evidence is a fleet transition measured in litres, kilometres and grams of CO₂ per consignment — reported to every contract customer each quarter.

  • HVO renewable diesel at all four hubs, cutting emissions up to 90% per litre.
  • 40 electric vans now covering urban routes that were previously diesel.
  • Load optimisation that removed 1.4 million empty kilometres from our network in 2024.
