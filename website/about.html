About Us

Who We Are

At Horizon Logistics, we connect businesses with reliable transportation and intelligent logistics solutions. Safety, transparency, efficiency, and customer satisfaction are at the heart of everything we do.

We are a European road freight and contract logistics operator with 185 vehicles, four distribution hubs and 640 colleagues. We are deliberately mid-sized: large enough to run complex supply chains, small enough that the person who plans your freight is the person you can call.

What We Stand For

Four principles, applied daily

These are not posters in the drivers' room. They are the questions we ask before we accept a load, buy a vehicle or hire a planner.

Safety first, always

A load that arrives late is a problem. A load that arrives unsafely is unacceptable. Our drivers stop when the conditions say stop.

Transparency

You see the same tracking we do. If a route slips, you hear it from us with the recovery plan attached.

Precision

Correct paperwork, correct temperature, correct window. The detail is the service.

Partnership

We would rather decline a contract than sign a service level we cannot hold for five years.

Our Story

Built one route at a time

Growth at Horizon has always followed the same pattern: a customer asks for one more country, and we build the capability properly before we sell it.

ISO 9001 ISO 14001 AEO Authorised GDP Compliant
1998

Three trucks in Rotterdam

Founded as a regional pallet carrier serving the port and the Westland growers.

2006

First cross-border network

Daily departures to Germany and Belgium, and the first 6,000 m² hub in Duisburg.

2014

Warehousing and contract logistics

Ambient and bonded storage added, with pick, pack and returns under one contract.

2019

Visibility platform

Every vehicle connected. Customers receive the same live position and ETA our planners see.

2025

Fleet transition

Euro 6 across the articulated fleet, HVO renewable diesel at all four hubs, and the first 40 electric vans.
